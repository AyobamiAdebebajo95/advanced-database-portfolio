## My Case Study: NaijaAutoMart Online Store

**What it is:** NaijaAutoMart is a made-up Nigerian online store. Sellers list products, customers buy them online, and the items are delivered to their homes.

**Who uses the database:**
- Customers – browse products, place orders, pay, leave reviews
- Sellers – add products and keep stock up to date
- Delivery agents – update delivery status
- Admins – manage the store and view sales reports

**What data it stores:** customers, sellers, products, stock, orders, payments, deliveries and reviews.

**Main things it does:**
- Add products and update stock
- Place an order and take payment
- Track deliveries
- Show sales reports
