## My Case Study: NaijaAutoMart Online Store

**What it is:** NaijaAutoMart is a made-up Nigerian online store for **cars and auto parts**. Dealers and parts sellers list new and used cars, tyres, batteries, engine parts and more. Customers buy online and get parts delivered, or book a car inspection before buying.

**Who uses the database:**
- Customers – search for cars and parts, check which parts fit their car, order, pay, leave reviews
- Sellers (car dealers and parts shops) – list cars and parts, keep stock up to date
- Delivery agents – deliver parts and update delivery status
- Admins – manage sellers and listings, view sales reports

**What data it stores:** customers, sellers, cars (make, model, year, mileage), auto parts, which parts fit which car models, stock, orders, payments, deliveries, inspection bookings and reviews.

**Main things it does:**
- List cars and parts and update stock
- Find parts that fit a customer's car
- Place an order and take payment
- Book a car inspection
- Track deliveries
- Show sales reports
